# Executive Summary

Cybersecurity governance requires measurable objectives to determine whether security controls are operating effectively. This report defines a set of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) designed to measure the effectiveness of technical controls, operational processes, and governance activities implemented within the organisation.

The KPIs support management by providing measurable targets, assigning ownership, and enabling continuous monitoring and improvement of the organisation's cybersecurity posture.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish measurable cybersecurity performance indicators that enable management to:

- Monitor the effectiveness of security controls.
- Measure organisational compliance.
- Improve operational security.
- Support informed risk-based decision making.
- Demonstrate continuous improvement.

---

# Scope

This KPI strategy applies to:

- Information Security
- Network Infrastructure
- Servers
- Workstations
- Network Devices
- IT Administrators
- Security Operations
- Incident Response Activities

---

# KPI Design Principles

The KPIs within this strategy are designed to be:

- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable
- Relevant
- Time-bound (SMART)

Each KPI includes:

- Measurement Method
- Performance Target
- Assigned Owner
- Review Frequency

# Enterprise KPI Register

## KPI 1

### Antivirus Compliance

**Objective**

Ensure all organisational systems maintain current antivirus protection.

**Measurement Method**

Review endpoint security reports and antivirus management dashboards.

**Target**

100% of systems updated monthly.

**Owner**

IT Security Administrator

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## KPI 2

### Unauthorised Access Attempts

**Objective**

Monitor unauthorised login attempts and suspicious authentication events.

**Measurement Method**

Review firewall logs, Syslog records, and failed login reports.

**Target**

Continuous monitoring with monthly reporting.

**Owner**

Security Administrator

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## KPI 3

### Incident Response Time

**Objective**

Measure the efficiency of incident response activities.

**Measurement Method**

Review incident response logs from detection through containment.

**Target**

Respond to high-priority incidents within 20 minutes.

**Owner**

Incident Response Team

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## KPI 4

### SSH Compliance

**Objective**

Ensure secure remote administration across all network devices.

**Measurement Method**

Review router and switch configurations to verify SSH is enabled and Telnet is disabled.

**Target**

100% of network devices secured with SSH.

**Owner**

Network Administrator

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## KPI 5

### Configuration Backup Success

**Objective**

Ensure network device configurations can be restored successfully.

**Measurement Method**

Review backup logs and perform restoration testing.

**Target**

100% successful backup and restoration verification.

**Owner**

System Administrator

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

# KPI Analysis

The implemented KPIs provide measurable visibility into the effectiveness of cybersecurity controls across the enterprise. Monitoring these indicators enables management to identify trends, evaluate compliance with organisational security objectives, and detect areas requiring corrective action.

The KPI set supports continuous governance by measuring endpoint protection, access control, incident response performance, secure remote administration, and backup reliability.

---

# Management Recommendations

Management should:

- Review KPI performance monthly.
- Investigate deviations from established targets.
- Allocate resources to underperforming areas.
- Review KPI relevance annually.
- Use KPI trends to guide cybersecurity investment decisions.

---

# Continuous Improvement Strategy

Cybersecurity governance should be reviewed continuously to ensure the organisation adapts to evolving threats and business requirements.

Future improvements may include:

- Multi-Factor Authentication compliance metrics.
- Security awareness training completion rates.
- Vulnerability remediation timelines.
- Patch management compliance.
- Third-party security assessment metrics.
- Cloud security governance KPIs.

---

# Conclusion

A structured cybersecurity KPI programme enables management to measure the effectiveness of security controls and governance activities through objective performance indicators. By assigning ownership, defining measurable targets, and conducting regular reviews, the organisation strengthens accountability, supports informed decision-making, and promotes continual improvement of its cybersecurity posture.
